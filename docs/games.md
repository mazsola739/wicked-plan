# Game catalogue

Every puzzle and game in Wicked Plan. Each game carries **the show's official English name** (as Netflix
uses it; the English Wikipedia article on The Devil's Plan lists them); the digital rules, art and texts
are our own.

Types: **Main** (everyone, the day's main game), **Mini team**, **Mini solo**, **Prison** (the losers' game),
**Hidden** (found and played alone), **Exile** (the way back for eliminated players), **Final duel** (one against one). Difficulty and Min. (the fewest seats the game is played with) come from each game's `GameInfo` (`difficulty`, `min_players`); the difficulty is one of five levels, from the owner's brief (1 easy, 2 normal, 3 medium, 4 hard, 5 expert), shown as e.g. "Medium 3/5" and colouring the card (green, blue, yellow, red, purple).

The exact rules of each digital version are on its page in `docs/games/<id>.md`; they adapt the short lines below.

## Main games

| Game | Difficulty | Min. | In short |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rules Race | hard | 4 | Board race where each player writes a personal rule; the die has prison and escape sides. |
| Zoo | medium | 3 | Bid chips for animal tiles on a 5×5 grid that fit your secret pattern. |
| Equation High-Low | hard | 3 | Build an equation from number and symbol cards, then bet on being closest to 1 or to 20. |
| Doubt and Bet | medium | 3 | Bet on how many cards of a colour are on the table; raise or call the bluff. |
| Unknown | hard | 4 | Race a cube to the centre square while working out the secret movement rules. |
| The Virus Game | hard | 6 | Hidden roles: terrorists spread a virus; citizens, officer, journalist and researchers stop it. |
| Either Or | normal | 3 | Ten two-way choices for points; four surprise mini games turn up along the way. |
| Crooked Cops | hard | 4 | Thieves collect hidden pieces on a metro map, cop teams hunt them, with secret corrupt cops. |
| Secret Number | hard | 4 | Everyone has a secret number from 1 to 100; trade clues, guess the others, protect your own. |
| Halloween Monster | hard | 4 | Form alliances, bid points, attack monsters with weapon cards and share the score. |
| Mystery Stock Market | expert | 3 | Trade made-up stocks over 24 rounds and work out the hidden rules that move the prices. |
| Laying Grass | medium | 2 | Grow your territory with polyomino pieces on a 30×30 grid; key squares give special moves. |
| Treasure Island | medium | 3 | Bid for arrows that open clue boxes on an 81-square island and find the treasure. |
| Balance Mancala | hard | 2 | Round mancala; your score is the lowest of your three colour totals. |
| Gem Black Market | hard | 4 | Everyone holds one hidden card (jewel or bomb); in two swap rounds trade with others, the buyer's 3 cards or the discard. Score when the buyer has your jewel; a bomb costs points. 3 rounds. |
| Auction Bingo | medium | 3 | A 3×3 bingo board of circles, triangles and stars. Over 7 rounds pick a shape in secret, then bid coins for it; who won stays secret. |

## Exile (the way back for eliminated players)

Eliminated players go to a hidden place. They can come back in two ways: crack the place's secrets and pass
the trial, or win the exile matches.

| Game | Difficulty | Min. | In short |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unknown Language | hard | 1 | Decode a made-up picture script (root + part-of-speech mark) and read the place's rules and how to open the trial. |
| Piece Puzzle | normal | 1 | A tangram-like piece puzzle, as a race. |
| Spot the Difference | easy | 1 | Spot the differences between two pictures, as a race. |
| Secret Code | hard | 1 | Code breaking, as a race. |
| Loop Fence Puzzle | medium | 1 | A fence-building logic puzzle, as a race. |
| Symmetrical Puzzle | normal | 1 | A symmetry puzzle, as a race. |
