## Season 1, Day 1: prison puzzle
- **Name in the source:** cast puzzle (in the prison)
- **Players:** the 2 prisoners, Players A and B
- **Stakes:** 1 piece for solving it before the next main match

### Rules on the show
- A cast-metal puzzle is provided in the prison.
- Solving it before the next main match starts earns one piece.

### How it went
- The two prisoners took turns working on it.

### Result
- Player A solved it and received 1 piece.

### Differences from our version
- Show: one physical cast puzzle, one deadline (the next main match), a piece as the reward.
- Ours: a linked-rings puzzle in three levels, scored in points by number of moves.
- Show: shared between the prisoners; ours is solo.

### Gaps
- The shape of the puzzle and what counts as solved are not described.
- Whether the piece goes only to the solver when both work on it is not stated as a rule (here the solver got it).

## Season 1, game-rules page
Source: the general and prison parts of the Season 1 game-rules page.

### Rules added
- Inmates get one cast puzzle, hidden somewhere in the prison for them to find; there is one puzzle per day of imprisonment.
- A different design is used every time.
- The reward is 1 piece, paid when the solved puzzle is handed to the dealer on leaving prison; the deadline is the end of the imprisonment (the next main match).
- The solved puzzle can be passed to the other inmate before release, and the reward goes with it.
- Puzzles by day, by name: Day 1 Enigma, Day 2 Hourglass, Day 3 Vortex (spelled "Vortax" in the translation), Day 4 Chain, Day 5 Trinity. The same note mentions the top difficulty grade, Grandmaster (six stars).

### Corrections to the day record
- Day 1 gap closed: the piece is not tied to who did the solving; it goes to whoever hands in the solved puzzle.
- Day 2 gap closed: it was not the Day 1 puzzle (Day 2: Hourglass).
- Day 3 has no cast-puzzle record, but the rules page lists a Day 3 puzzle (Vortex). The page does not say whether it was solved.
- Day 4: puzzle was Chain; the reward is the standard 1 piece.
- Day 5: puzzle was Trinity; the hand-over of the reward is allowed by the rules, and the amount is 1 piece.
- Day 5 gap partly closed: reward amount and deadline are now known.

### Gaps
- The shapes of the puzzles and what counts as solved (taken apart, or also reassembled) are not described.
- Whether each puzzle named is of the Grandmaster grade, or only some, is unclear in translation.
- The page's two wordings differ ("a piece to each player" against "a piece to the one holding the puzzle"); the second, with the footnote on handing it over, is read here as one piece per solved puzzle.
