## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 6: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Gradation / "Gradient" (6th prison match)
- **Players:** 3, playing one-against-one matches in turn
- **Stakes:** the first to win two matches in a row goes to the final; the other two are eliminated

### Rules on the show
- Each player has 7 cube containers shaded from dark at both ends to light in the middle, and 20 number cubes (two each of 0 to 9).
- Before a match both players secretly put one cube into each of their 7 containers, within 5 minutes.
  - The seven numbers add up to at most 30.
  - They must be in ascending order from left to right (equal neighbours occur).
- Lots decide who plays the first match and who leads.
- A match is 7 rounds. The leader puts forward one container, then the opponent answers with one. The opponent sees only the shade, not the container's position in the original order. Both cubes are revealed.
- The higher number wins the round and 1 point. The round winner leads the next round. On a draw nobody scores and the lead stays.
- After 7 rounds the higher score takes the match; a tied match is replayed until there is a winner.
- The match winner then plays the third player, and leads the first round.
- This goes on until someone wins two matches in a row.
