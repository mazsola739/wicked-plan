## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 6: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Equation Pyramid (also "Formula Pyramid")
- **Players:** 2, head to head: Player A and Player B.
- **Stakes:** the winner survives, moves to the living quarters and takes the second place in the final; the loser is eliminated. No piece reward.

### Rules on the show
- 10 rounds. Each round shows a pyramid of 10 cells (rows of 1, 2, 3 and 4, lettered A to J) and a target number.
- Every cell holds one operator (plus, minus, times, divide) and a number.
- A solution is 3 cells in a chosen order that make the target. The operator of the first cell is ignored; its number is the starting value.
- Normal order of operations: times and divide before plus and minus.
- A player who finds one buzzes and names the three cell letters, with 10 seconds to answer.
- Right answer: +1 point. Wrong answer or no answer in time: -1. Naming an answer already given: -1.
- A round ends after 3 minutes, or earlier when all solutions have been found.
- Most points after round 10 wins.
- The players face each other, each reading a screen of their own placed over the opponent.

- A noticed that the opponent could see A while reading the screen, and from around round 4 moved the body, stared and walked about to distract B. B later said this had a strong effect.

### Result
- Player A won 16 to 11 and became the second finalist. Player B was eliminated.

### Differences from our version
- Show: a duel with buzzers. Ours: solo.
- Show: 10 rounds with a 3-minute limit each. Ours: 5 rounds, no time limit.
- Show: each cell carries its own operator, including divide. Ours: plain number cards 1 to 9; the player chooses plus, minus or times.
- Show: normal order of operations. Ours: left to right.
- Show: several solutions can be claimed per round, +1 / -1. Ours: one answer per round, 100 for a right one.

### Gaps
- "The same cells cannot be declared" is garbled; read here as: an answer already given cannot score again. Whether the same three cells in another order count as a new answer is implied by the log but not stated.
- Whether one cell may be used twice is not said (no logged answer does).
- No tie-break is given.
- The move log's correct and wrong labels are unreliable in translation; the running scores are the guide's.
- Whether division must come out whole is not said.

## Season 2 (Death Room), game-rules page
Source: the game-rules page has no block for this game; these points come from a re-reading of the rule block, notes and answer log of the Day 6 page.

### Rules added
- The order of the three cells counts: in round 1 one order of a set of cells was judged wrong and another order of the same cells right.
- There is no call for declaring that no answers remain.

### Corrections to the day record
- The garbled rule "the same cells cannot be declared" most likely means that one cell cannot be used twice within an answer. Repeating an answer already given is covered separately, by the sentence that sets the point penalty. This answers the gap on reusing a cell, with the caution that it rests on a machine translation.

### Gaps
- Whether another correct order of an already answered set of cells scores again is not said.
- No tie-break is given.
- Whether division must come out whole is not said.
