## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 2: living-quarters hidden stage
- **Name in the source:** Knight's Journey (Knight's Tour)
- **Players:** 1 volunteer from the living quarters, Player A
- **Stakes:** success gives a benefit useful in the final rounds. Failure does not eliminate, but costs all pieces except one, and the player returns to the living quarters. One player only, one chance.

### Rules on the show
- Entry: a piece is placed fully into a hole of a wall device in the gym; a hidden white room opens, holding one piece (which repays the cost) and an information screen.
- A knight moves in the chess L shape and must visit every square of the board.
- Three boards in order: 6×6, 7×7, 8×8, with one 60-minute limit for all three.
- If the knight is stuck before every square is visited, the moves are cleared and that board starts again. Retries are unlimited within the time.
- The puzzle was available to practise in the living quarters from Day 1 (the prison had it too).

### How it went
- Players had practised overnight on Day 1; A mastered 7×7 during the Day 2 prison match and then went in.
- Moving a piece on the large board felt unlike practising on paper, but 6×6 and 7×7 fell within about five minutes.
- 8×8 had not been practised. A used a pattern another player had taught and marked the route with scraps of wax paper brought along; after restarting, A finished.

### Result
- Remaining time: 57:57 after 6×6, 54:56 after 7×7; 8×8 was last reset at 51:27 and solved with 37 seconds left.
- Success. The reward, kept secret then and revealed on Day 5: the right to claim 10 extra pieces once called for, at any time and place except in the prison match room, even when the player has no pieces left.

### Differences from our version
- Show boards 6×6, 7×7, 8×8 in 60 minutes; ours 5×5, 6×6, 7×7 in 15 minutes.
- All or nothing on the show, with pieces at stake; ours scores points per finished board plus a time bonus.
- Shared: knight moves, three boards on one clock, restarting a stuck board.

### Gaps
- Whether the starting square is free or fixed is not said.
- The page does not say whether the 10-piece right can be used more than once.

## Season 2 (Death Room), game-rules page
Source: the hidden-stage part of the game-rules page, plus the Day 1 and Day 2 pages it points to (folded text included). It covers the Day 2 living-quarters hidden stage.

### Rules added
- **Route in:** each bedroom of the living building has a faulty table clock. Its back holds a five-letter word lock and a peephole onto a letter die. The letters seen spell "juliet", and the five-letter answer is "Romeo". This opens the clock, which holds a hex wrench and a note about making the bird on a tree fly.
- The note points to a tree picture on a wall. The wrench frees it and uncovers four piece slots with line patterns around them. A piece set halfway into the second slot from the left, the right way round, completes the patterns into the word "INSERT"; handing a piece into that slot opens the secret door. A piece inside the white room repays it.
- **Stage notice:** one player only, one chance only; success puts that player in a very favourable position for the final; failure does not eliminate. Anyone not challenging is asked to leave.
- **Reward text:** 10 pieces, paid at once, wherever the holder is, when the holder announces the wish to collect them. It can be claimed even after a game has left the holder with 0 pieces, but not in the death room.
- The knight's tour also lay in both wings as a solo board game from Day 1.

### Corrections to the day record
- Gap "whether the 10-piece right can be used more than once": the reward text describes one payment of 10 pieces, made when claimed. Nothing offers a second claim.

### Gaps
- Whether the starting square on each board is free or fixed is still not said.
- The game-rules page gives no board rules or time limit; those come from the Day 2 page.
