## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 7: final, game 3
- **Name in the source:** Equation Traffic Light / "Formula Traffic Light" / "Mathematical Traffic Light"
- **Players:** the 2 finalists
- **Stakes:** deciding game of the final (one game each before it)

### Rules on the show
- **Secret:** within 5 minutes each player builds a 9-cell formula and hands it in privately.
  - Digits 0 to 9 and + - × ÷ = ^; symbols may be used more than once.
  - It must be true and contain at least one =.
  - Not allowed: symbols next to each other, numbers starting with 0, implied multiplication.
- **Guess:** within 5 minutes a player enters 9 cells of numbers and symbols. A guess need not follow the rules for secrets.
- **Colours**, per cell, against the opponent's secret:
  - Red: that digit or symbol is not in it.
  - Yellow: it is in it, in another place.
  - Green: right place.
  - Copies beyond the number in the secret are red; exact matches are counted green first.
- No guess in time: no colours that turn.
- **Win:** all 9 cells green. If both manage it at the same time, a rematch until one wins alone.

### How it went
G = green, Y = yellow, R = red.

| Turn | A's guess | Colours | B's guess | Colours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `0×+−÷12^3` | R R Y R R Y Y Y R | `34=5×6+78` | R R Y R Y R R R R |
| 2 | `4^5+78+96` | G Y Y R Y R G R R | `9^21÷−=20` | G Y R Y R R G R Y |
| 3 | `11^2=5+77` | Y R Y G Y G G Y R | `9^1×10=91` | G Y Y Y R G G G G |
| 4 | `47=2^5+15` | all G | `91^1×0=91` | G G Y R Y G G G G |

- A opened with a deliberately invalid line to test many symbols at once.
- B learned in turn 1 that the other secret used none of the digits 3 to 8, an early advantage.
- B's last guess could not be a true equation: it rested on a mistake about powers and the order of operations (the true secret needs 9 to the power 0).
- A had asked the dealer about the order of operations during the game.

### Result
- A solved `47=2^5+15` (B's secret) on turn 4 and won. A's secret was `91×9^0=91`.
- A took the final 2:1 and became champion; prize 200 million won.
