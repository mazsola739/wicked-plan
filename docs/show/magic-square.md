# Divine Test on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/magic-square.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source

## Season 3 (Dead Again), Exile (Days 4 and 5): hidden stage, God's test
- **Name in the source:** Test of God / "49 magic square"
- **Players:** one exile at a time
- **Stakes:** pass: return to the next main match, with a choice of 10 pieces or 1 prize-money token (adds 100 million won). Fail: final elimination. Once someone passes, nobody else may try

### Rules on the show
- Fill a 7 × 7 board with number tiles 1 to 49, written in the numerals of the exile language, so that it is a magic square (rows, columns and diagonals with equal sums).
- One tile is fixed in advance: 1, in the middle of the top row.
- 1 hour, counted by an hourglass from the moment the player enters.
- Passed as soon as the correct square is in place within the time.
- The trial can be attempted before a main match begins, after opening the way as the carvings describe. The carvings (area 4) are the only information on it; there is no separate rulebook.

### How it went
- While preparing, the exiles guessed from the carvings that it would be a magic square and practised with small ones.
- Day 4: Player A opened the way and went in first. After an hour came the news that A had failed; A was finally eliminated.
- Player B was then next. Player C offered to teach B a full construction method in exchange for B's turn; B declined. C was allowed to shuffle the rule tiles of the exile place afterwards, and the two practised.
- B passed, using partly trial and error, combining A's algorithm with C's teaching. The finished square matches the classic "Siamese" construction: from 1 at top centre, place each next number up and to the right, wrapping round the edges; if that cell is taken, go one cell down instead.
- B chose the 10 pieces and returned to the main game on Day 5.

### Result
A failed and was eliminated; B passed, took 10 pieces and returned. The trial then closed.

### Differences from our version
- Show: one 7 × 7 board, 1 hour, only the 1 given. Ours: 3 × 3, 4 × 4 and 5 × 5 with 2, 5 and 10 minutes and a few numbers given (up to six).
- Show: one attempt, elimination on failure. Ours: checks cost nothing, and a failed or skipped board only scores 0.
- Ours scores points per board plus a time bonus; the show gave pieces or a prize token and a return to the game.

### Gaps
- The source does not say clearly when B's attempt took place (it is reported at the start of Day 5).
- How correctness is judged during the hour (checks allowed or not) is not said.
