## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 4: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Triple Dice
- **Players:** 4 from the prison quarters (Players A, B, C, D), each for themselves
- **Stakes:** the highest total wins 1 piece; the lowest total is eliminated

### Rules on the show
- 4 rounds. Each player has 9 white dice, 1 blue die and 1 red die.
- At the start of a round everyone rolls the 9 white dice in the open. Then each rolls the blue and red dice through dice towers of the same colour; only the owner sees these two hidden dice.
- Prediction: starting with the first player, each secretly picks a card for the points they will earn this round: ZERO (0), MIN (above 0, up to 7), MORE (above 7, up to 10) or MAX (above 10).
- Three turns per round. On each, players in turn submit three of their dice; a hidden die is submitted with its colour marker and revealed after everyone has submitted. The two dice left over are dropped.
- Combinations from high to low: triple; straight (three in a row, 6 and 1 do not connect); double; single (none of these).
- Ties: the higher number forming the combination wins, then the higher remaining number; identical sets share the rank.
- Points per turn: 6, 3, 1, 0 for first to fourth; tied players get the average of the places they share.
- The first player of round 1 is set by a die roll. From the second turn, whoever ranked highest on the previous one goes first; if tied, the one with more total points.
- A correct prediction doubles that round's points. A correct ZERO instead earns a flat 40 bonus.

### How it went
- Round 1 (predictions: B MAX, D MAX, C MORE, A ZERO): B opened with triple sixes. A played as if unwell and confused, misusing hidden dice, and ended with 0 points, which the others only then recognised as a successful ZERO. Round points: B 12, C 10, D 8, A 0. Totals: A 40, B 24, C 20, D 8.
- Round 2 (B MAX, D MORE, C MIN, A MIN): others suspected another ZERO from A, but all four predictions came true, with B and C sharing a turn on equal triple sixes. Round points: B 11, D 10, C 6, A 3. Totals: A 46, B 46, C 32, D 28.
- Round 3 (B MORE, D ZERO, C MORE, A MIN): D, far behind, played low hands to chase ZERO. C read this and played 1-2-4, the weakest set, so that D had to score. Round points: B 11.5, C 8, A 6.5, D 4. Totals: A 59, B 57.5, C 48, D 32.
- Round 4 (predictions as listed: B ZERO, D ZERO, C MIN, A MORE): D needed ZERO to catch up and got through the first turn. On the second, A broke up a low set while going for first place, and B passed up the chance to block D by playing a straight. It fell to C, who played 1-3-5 and took last place, pushing D up to third and ending D's ZERO. Round points: A 15, B 8, C 6, D 1.

### Result
- Final totals: A 74, B 65.5, C 60, D 33.
- A won 1 piece. D was eliminated (and D's pieces vanished with the elimination). B and C survived.

### Differences from our version
- Show: 11 dice per player (9 open white, 2 hidden), from which three sets of three are chosen; ours: 3 dice (open, secret, blind) with no choosing.
- Show: no die is hidden from its own owner and there is no reroll; ours has a blind die and one reroll of the secret die.
- Show: prediction of the round's point band (ZERO, MIN, MORE, MAX); ours: prediction of your rank.
- Show: 6/3/1/0 per turn, doubled on a correct prediction, ZERO worth 40; ours: +3 exact, +1 off by one, +1 for the best hand.
- Show: 4 rounds of 3 turns in turn order; ours: 6 rounds, simultaneous.
- Hand order (triple, straight, pair, nothing) is the same; the show breaks ties on the combination's number first, ours on the sum.

### Gaps
- The band limits are garbled in translation; the limits above fit every result shown.
- In round 4 the guide's table gives B a ZERO prediction, which fits poorly with how B played; B's prediction failed either way.
- Some hidden dice were never shown, and parts of the dice tables are garbled.
- Whether the prediction cards were revealed only at the end of the round is not stated.
