are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 5: main match
- **Name in the source:** Balance Mancala
- **Players:** 7, each for themselves (alliances allowed). Player A to Player G, lettered in turn order.
- **Stakes:** first place took 5 pieces from last place (all of them if last place held fewer than 5); anyone below the score bar lost half of their pieces. The pieces then decided who slept in the living quarters and who in the prison. A prize-money mission ran inside the match.

### Rules on the show
- **Board:** a ring of 14 spaces: 12 coloured spaces plus a black devil space and a white angel space on opposite sides. Clockwise from the devil: spaces 1 to 6, the angel, spaces 7 to 12, back to the devil. The colours repeat red, blue, green from space 1 (1 red, 2 blue, 3 green, and so on to 12 green). (The numbering is the guide's own, for description.)
- **Markers:** every player owns 4 markers (28 in all). The scores come in three colours: red, blue, green.
- **Setup:** turn order is drawn by lot. In that order players put down their markers one at a time, at most 2 per space. In the broadcast game every space, devil and angel included, started with exactly 2.
- **Turn:** pick a space that holds at least one of your markers, pick up every marker on it (yours and others') and drop them one per space clockwise, in any order you like. The devil and angel spaces are part of the path.
- **Scoring a move:** only the owner of the last marker dropped scores, whoever made the move. On a coloured space they gain that colour, as many points as there are markers on the space (the last one included).
- **Angel space:** the owner gains that many points in their lowest colour.
- **Devil space:** the owner gains that many points in their highest colour.
- If the lowest colour is tied, the player making the move chooses which colour gets the points.
- Scores are public and tallied during play.
- **End:** as soon as any player reaches the 30-point limit in one colour (see Gaps).
- **Final score:** lowest colour total minus (highest colour total minus lowest), that is 2 x lowest - highest. Example: 13 / 11 / 20 gives 2.
- **Penalty:** a final score of 15 or less costs half of the player's pieces (the loss is rounded down).
- **Ranking reward:** the top scorer takes 5 pieces from the bottom scorer.
- No pieces are spent during play.
- **Prize-money mission** (announced after round 5, called the last one of the season): five times in total, move another player's marker onto the devil space so that its owner gains 5 or more points at once.

### How it went
- Pieces at the start: A 8, B 2, C 11, D 2, E 8, F 13, G 4.
- Before play Player B, from the prison group, gathered a majority against the living-quarters players. Player C and Player E joined, and Player E told Player A and Player F openly that they would oppose them. It looked like 5 against 2 (A and F). In secret Player D had brought Player F into the majority, so it was 6 against 1.
- Rounds 1 to 5: the majority collected points and emptied the spaces from which A and F could score. In round 4 A passed up own points to give F some, and D quietly talked to F.
- Rounds 6 to 8: the majority chased the mission (1 time in round 6, 1 in round 7, 3 in round 8). In round 6 F turned down A's idea of emptying the devil space; in round 7, with A's agreement, F granted D's request to be sent there. In round 8 A spread out the large pile on the angel space to disrupt the majority's score control. The mission was completed (5/5), but several majority players now had one colour far above the others.
- Round 9: A asked F to feed green to B and offered F the pieces A would get for winning. F refused and openly joined the majority, to keep A (who held an unrevealed hidden-stage reward) from surviving.
- Round 10: A, now alone, asked C and E to change sides. Both did, mainly out of sympathy, after an argument with B and G. A truce was agreed (the guide credits the proposal both to D and to B): the old alliance's plan would hold until the round ended, with one adjusted move for E. It then became 3 (A, C, E) against 4 (B, D, F, G).
- Round 11: the four aimed to push E, already high in green, to the 30 limit so that the game would end with E last. B gave E 5 green (27) and C kept E short of the limit. D took a blue point and moved into first. E then moved a large stack in a way that opened a chain: F added a marker to one space, and G sowed that stack so that E's marker ended on the devil space. E gained 5 green (32) and the game ended.

- Every player was below the bar, so all lost half of their pieces.
- Player E, left with no pieces, was eliminated.
- Right after the housing announcement Player A claimed a living-quarters hidden-stage reward (10 pieces, usable at any time) and rose to 14. A, F and C stayed in the living quarters; D, the match winner, was moved to the prison with B and G.
- The mission succeeded; the cumulative prize money stood at 380 million won.

### Differences from our version
- Show: one shared ring of 14 coloured and special spaces. Ours: a row of 5 pits and a store per player.
- Show: markers belong to players and the colour comes from the space. Ours: stones are red, green or blue and belong to whoever's pit holds them.
- Show: 7 players. Ours: 2 to 4 (you and 3 bots).
- Show: angel and devil spaces, last marker's owner scores. Ours: stores, extra turns and captures.
- Show: any drop order, marker by marker. Ours: choose a first colour, then the other colours follow in a fixed order.
- Show: score is 2 x lowest - highest. Ours: 50 x lowest colour + all stones.
- Show: ends at the 30-point limit. Ours: ends when the player to move has no stones, or after 40 turns each.
- Show: half-pieces penalty, transfer from last to first, prize mission. Ours: none.

### Gaps
- The end threshold is described both as scoring more than 30 and as reaching 30 in one colour; the final move took the colour to 32.
- The penalty bar is given as "15 or less", while the guide's strategy note says three colours of 15 each avoid it; whether a score of exactly 15 is penalised is not certain.
- No maximum number of rounds is given.
- The rule that the chosen space must hold your own marker is garbled in translation; every logged move agrees with it.
- Only the tie among lowest colours is covered; a tie among highest colours on the devil space is not.
- Ties for first or last place are not covered.
- Player A's final score is 3 in the result table, but the move log's last totals give 2.
- The money value of the mission is not stated, only the cumulative total.
