How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/tiger-and-goat.md).

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 7: final, game 2
- **Name in the source:** Bagchal (Bagh Chal)
- **Players:** the 2 finalists. Player A (14 pieces), Player B (11 pieces).
- **Stakes:** second game of the final. Piece benefit (for A): choose who moves first.

### Rules on the show
- The usual Bagh Chal board of 5 x 5 points (columns A to E, rows 1 to 5) joined by lines.
- **Two boards played together:** each player is the goats on one board and the tigers on the other.
- Per board: 4 tigers start on the corners; 20 goats start off the board. Goats move first.
- **Goat move:** place one goat; once all 20 are placed, move one goat along a line.
- **Tiger move:** move one tiger one step along a line, or jump over an adjacent goat to the empty point behind it and remove that goat.
- **Turn order:** the first player makes one move, as goats on their goat board. After that each turn is two moves, one on each board: the other player answers as tigers on that board and then moves as goats on the other, and so on back and forth.
- Time limit of 90 seconds.
- **Win:** the tigers win a board by capturing 5 goats; the goats win it if the tigers cannot move. The first board to finish ends the whole game, and its winner wins.
- Before the game each finalist had 30 minutes of practice with one eliminated player of their choice.

### How it went
- A picked a practice partner first; B picked another.
- A appears to have moved first (goats on the first board).
- Both began placing goats from the edge; the two finalists differed on how urgent the corner points were.
- Around move 43 of the game record, B as goats put a goat where A's tiger could capture it at once, hoping to trap the tiger afterwards while there was still much open space.
- B then kept giving up goats while trying to save the formation, and A reached the capture target before any trap was formed.

### Result
- Player A won the board where A played tigers, and so the game (final: 1 to 1).

### Differences from our version
- Show: the first finished board decides the game. Ours: both boards are played to the end and points decide.
- Show: turns alternate between the boards, with the goat moves leading on one board first. Ours: steps in which goats move on both boards, then tigers on both.
- Show: 90-second time limit. Ours: no timer, but a board ends after 100 turns.
- Setup, goat and tiger moves and the 5-capture target match.

### Gaps
- Whether the 90 seconds apply to one move or to a two-move turn is not clear.
- A repetition or deadlock rule was not explained; the producer later mentioned a reserve game for a stalemate.
- The move-by-move boards are pictures in the guide and are not transcribed.
- The turn-order sentence is garbled; the order above is the reading that fits the guide's footnote and move numbers.
