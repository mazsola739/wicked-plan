## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 3: main match
- **Name in the source:** Jewel Black Market (Gem Black Market)
- **Players:** 9, each for themselves, with a hidden one-way "Secret Santa" link between players. In practice a 5-player alliance from the living quarters (Players A, E, G, H, I) faced a 4-player alliance from the prison (Players B, C, D, F).
- **Stakes:** places 1-3 to the living quarters, places 4-8 to the prison, last place eliminated. Final score of 1 or more: 1 piece; 10 or more: 2 pieces. First place also got the Alliance Token.

### Rules on the show
- **Seating:** a draw sets a fixed base order. Turns run in that order, starting from the round's first player and wrapping round.
- **First player of Round 1:** a secret piece auction. The highest bid wins and pays; losing bids are returned. Tied top bidders bid again among themselves.
- **Deck, 13 cards:** Diamond 5, 6; Gold 4, 5; Silver 2, 3, 4; Bronze 1, 1, 2, 2; Bombs -6, -4.
- **3 rounds.** Each round: shuffle, deal 1 face-down card to each player from the first player on; of the 4 cards left, 3 go to the buyer's space and 1 to the trash.
- Each player looks at their card in turn order at the start (behind partitions). After that they may look at their own card only during their own turn.
- **Two exchanges per round:** the first in forward order, the second in reverse order (so the first player acts first and last). Time limit 3 minutes per exchange.
- On a turn the player must exchange their card with another player's card, a buyer's card or the trash card. Passing is not allowed.
- A player holding a bomb must exchange it with another player.
- **Bomb bonus:** when another player, on that player's turn, takes the bomb you hold, you gain 3 points at once. Passing a bomb away on your own turn earns nothing.
- **Peek:** once per game a player may pay 1 piece to look at one buyer's card or the trash card.
- **Round scoring** (all cards revealed): a jewel scores its number if the buyer's space holds a card of the same type, multiplied by the count of that type there. A bomb subtracts its number. Totals are shown after every round.
- Scoring points with a diamond also gives 1 piece immediately.
- **First player of Rounds 2 and 3:** whoever ended the previous round with the -6 bomb; if no player has it, the -4 holder; if no player holds a bomb, it stays the same.
- **Secret Santa:** before the game a draw places all players in one closed one-way chain. In the dealer room each player learns whose score they affect, but not who affects theirs.
- **Final score** = own points x the points of one's Secret Santa. Ranking is by final score.
- **Tie-breaks:** higher own points; if still equal, the player who acted earlier in the second exchange of Round 3.

### How it went
- **Preparation:** Players A, C, E and H tied in the piece auction with nothing more to bid, so the ruling was that the tied players had to agree on a winner. After about an hour and a half they settled on Player H, who paid 3 pieces. Player D received a Token of Salvation in the dealer room during this phase.
- **Chain** (arrow: the left player's points multiply the right player's final score): A > H > C > F > B > E > D > I > G > A.
- **Round 1:** both alliances kept a bomb in play and traded it among allies to collect the +3. The prison side adopted a plan from Player F: gather both bombs so that chosen players end on negative points and drag down those they multiply. They ended the round with both bombs (B -6, F -4). After a muddled talk on the living side under time pressure, Player H's last swap put a diamond into the buyer's space, which let Player D score with a diamond and earn a piece. Totals: D 12, A 9, G 5, I 3, C 3, E 2, H 0, F -4, B -6.
- **Round 2:** Player B started. Players D, B, C and later F paid to peek. Player A worked out the whole chain and the prison plan from the Round 1 record. The living side held both bombs at the start and passed points to Players B and F to weaken their negative scores. Player I stayed anxious about being the target and doubted the alliance. Totals: A 16, G 13, I 11, D 11, E 5, H 3, C 3, F -1, B -6.
- **Round 3:** Players I, E and G peeked. The living side could save Player E by lifting F's own score above E's, or both B's and F's above zero; the prison side needed B and F negative while E scored high. The living side fed bomb points to F. On turn 16 Player D swapped with the wrong player; Player C suspected betrayal for a moment, then saw it was a slip. Player B tracked every card and on the very last turn took E's -6 bomb in exchange for a diamond: E gained 3 for the bomb and 5 for the diamond, which raised the negative product on E, while B ended at -12.

### Differences from our version
- Ours seats 4-8 (page: 4); the show had 9, so all 13 cards were in play every round.
- No Secret Santa multiplication in ours; the show's final score was own points times another player's points.
- No bomb bonus in ours; on the show a bomb taken from you by another player was worth +3.
- Ours allows "keep"; on the show every turn was a forced exchange.
- Ours lets both players see their new cards after a swap; on the show a player could look at their own card only during their own turn (after the opening look).
- No paid peek and no diamond piece reward in ours.
- First player: ours moves one seat each round; the show used a piece auction, then the bomb holder.
- Ties and rewards: ours gives rank points; the show gave pieces, residences, the Alliance Token and one elimination.
- The show had a 3-minute limit per exchange; our page states none.

### Gaps
- The bomb-bonus sentence is garbled in the translation. The reading above agrees with the source's footnote and with the round totals in its exchange log.
- One row of the Round 1 exchange table (turn 3) appears to have its two card columns swapped.
- One cumulative table seems to show Player H's final own points with a minus sign; the final products only work with +5.
- The source gives the 5-player and 4-player alliance sizes in the preparation section but mixes the two numbers up elsewhere.
- Bid amounts in the auction are not listed (the winner paid 3).
- The tie-break wording about the Round 3 exchange order is unclear in translation.
- Not stated whether a player sees the received card immediately when taking a buyer's or trash card.
