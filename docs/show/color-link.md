- **Name in the source:** Color Link
- **Players:** the 2 finalists
- **Stakes:** second of the three final games

### Rules on the show
- 28 pieces in 14 colours (marked A to N), two per colour.
- A different board each round; black (X) and white (Y) pieces may be placed on it beforehand.
- A coin toss decides the order in round 1; later, the loser of the previous round chooses the order, within 3 minutes.
- The first placement of a round is 1 piece by the first player. After that each turn places 2 pieces of different colours, within 3 minutes; running out of time loses the round.
- **Condition:** each pair of one colour must be joinable by a path of at least 6 squares, counting the two end squares, and the paths of all colours together must neither overlap nor cross.
- **Declaring impossibility:** after the opponent's placement, a player who thinks the condition can no longer be met may declare it. The player who just placed must then show within 5 minutes that all colours on the board can be joined without overlap; for a colour with only one piece on the board, the partner is placed too and joined.
- Successful proof: 1 point to the prover. Failed proof: 1 point to the declarer.
- Up to 5 rounds; the first to 3 points wins the game.
