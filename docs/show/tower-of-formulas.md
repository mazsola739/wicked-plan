## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 5: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Tower of Formulas / Tower of Equations (5th prison match)
- **Players:** the 3 prisoners, each for themselves
- **Stakes:** the lowest score is eliminated; 1st place gets 3 pieces

### Rules on the show
- Stacked number blocks and formula (operator) blocks. Each round the screen shows a **target number** and a **target number of floors**: build a formula tower of that many blocks whose value is the target (example: target 25 with 4 floors).
- The formula is read from the top floor downwards. The top block must be a plain number block without an operator.
- **A move:** take any number of blocks off the top of a stack, as one group, and put them anywhere. The group may be flipped while moving; flipping it and setting it back in the same place also counts as one move.
- **Bidding:** within 5 minutes of the problem appearing, a player may buzz and name a number of moves in which they can solve it. The first buzz ends the 5 minutes and gives the others 2 more minutes to name a lower number. Each player may bid only once.
- If nobody buzzes, the problem is void and the next round starts.
- Only the lowest bidder builds: within 2 minutes, in exactly the number of moves named.
- **Scoring:** the player who bid in the first 5 minutes gets +1 for success, -1 for failure; a player who bid in the extra 2 minutes gets +2 or -2.
- The next round brings a new target and floor count; the blocks stay as they were left.
- **End:** when someone reaches 7 points. The lowest score is eliminated; a tie for lowest goes to overtime until someone reaches 7.

### How it went
- At first all three lost points through confusion over the rule that the top block must be a plain number; after that it stayed very close.
- A struggled early (points such as -1, -2, 0). With A on 5 and the others on 6 each, A solved two problems in a row, reached 7 and was safe first.
- B and C went to overtime. C buzzed too hastily and got it wrong; B then worked through the moves step by step and got the answer.

### Result
- A first to 7, survives. B survives. C eliminated.
