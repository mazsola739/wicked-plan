# Halloween Monster on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/monster-alliance.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source

A **game-rules page** section comes after the day sections: where it adds to or corrects

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 3: main match
- **Name in the source:** Halloween Monster
- **Players:** 11. Three alliances of three formed: trio X (Players A, B, C), trio Y (Players D, E, F), trio Z (Players G, H, I); Players J and K played solo.
- **Stakes:** the player or players with the most final victory points share 6 pieces; five players go to the living quarters and the rest to prison. Prize mission: 100 million won if one player is eliminated when the match ends.

### Rules on the show
Setup
- Everyone starts with 5 victory points and a basic weapon card, the Dagger. Players bring their own pieces.
- Before the game, players have 60 minutes to register alliances with the dealer, at most three members, one alliance per player. A registration cannot be cancelled or undone. Anyone not registered when time runs out plays solo.

Monsters
- Monsters wait in a queue; three stand on the battlefields (numbered 1 to 3, marked with a double line). Each has health shown as hearts, which is also the victory points it is worth, plus loot (pieces and/or special weapons).
- Thirteen monsters, in queue order (health: loot): 1 Jack-o'-lantern (5: poison); 2 tree monster (9: grenade); 3 witch (15: time bomb, piece); 4 Dracula (10: ice, dual swords); 5 werewolf (5: grenade); 6 mummy (7: grenade, poison); 7 Frankenstein (9: time bomb, dual swords); 8 zombie (13: ice, grenade); 9 gargoyle (9: time bomb); 10 clown (12: grenade); 11 goblin (18: grenade); 12 Medusa (15: piece); 13 Reaper (health and loot hidden until it enters the queue; then 20 health, 2 pieces).

A round
1. Point transfer: allies enter the dealer room together, up to 20 minutes, and move victory points among themselves. Each must keep at least 1. Changes are allowed until they leave the room, not after.
2. Attack order: highest victory points goes first. Ties in the first round are drawn by lot; from the second round, the tied player who went earlier in the previous round goes later.
3. Weapon choice (30 minutes): each player enters the dealer room, within 2 minutes picks one battlefield and one weapon card. Attacking is compulsory every round.
4. Resolution: attacks resolve automatically in order. While choosing, players are not shown the monsters' remaining health or others' choices.

Damage and kills
- The attack that takes a monster to 0 health kills it; extra damage is lost. That player alone gets its victory points and loot.
- A dead monster leaves at once and the next one in the queue fills the slot. Later players who chose that slot hit the new monster.
- The Dagger returns to its owner; special weapons are discarded after use.
- Weapons: Dagger 3 damage. Poison 1 damage, then 1 more on every player's turn until the target dies. Ice 3 damage, then the target takes no damage until the user's next turn. Dual swords 4 on one battlefield, or 2 each on two. Grenade 6. Time bomb 10, going off on that battlefield at the user's turn in the following round.
- Order of effects in a turn: ice wearing off, then the weapon's damage, then time bomb, then poison.

Items and trade
- Bought from the dealer at any time for 1 piece each: Scout (used at point transfer; shows the health of the monsters on the battlefields for that round) and Change Order (used when submitting a weapon; moves your turn to a position you choose).
- Special weapons and items may be given or traded, also across alliances. A lost one is not replaced.

Hidden rule
- The attack-order slots 10 and 11 carry the same double line as the monster battlefields: they are battlefields too, and the players standing there can be attacked.
- Killing a player gives the killer their victory points, pieces and special weapons. Losing all pieces this way eliminates the victim.

End and scoring
- The game ends when every monster from the queue and the battlefields is dead.
- Each alliance's victory points are added up and divided equally among its members (to one decimal).
- The highest final score wins 6 pieces; tied leaders split them equally and any remainder is discarded.

### How it went
Preparation
- Player A had suggested to trio Z a nine-player bloc of all three trios. Player D preferred six (X and Y), and the bloc was formed that way, planning to leave the two solo players as the elimination candidates. Trio Z felt betrayed but could not leave its registration.
- Player D falsely told Player E which hidden stage D had played; E found out in round 1.

Round 1 (order: B 13, E 13, I, J, G, H, K at 5, then F, C, D, A at 1)
- The bloc split each trio's points 13/1/1 to hold both ends of the order. Trio Z hesitated and kept points even.
- J guessed trio Z's target and set up the kills: E took the Jack-o'-lantern, G the tree monster, K the werewolf, A Dracula.

Round 2 (order: A 23, E 18, K 10, H, G, I at 8, J 5, then D, C, F, B at 1)
- Z kept its points equal so the solo players would not get a useful position. H offered the solo players a special weapon to hit the witch; J asked for pieces later instead, H dropped the idea, and J attacked the witch anyway.
- A poisoned the witch and E froze Frankenstein, wasting later attacks on it. D killed the witch (with a piece and a time bomb) and B the mummy.

Round 3 (order: E 30, C 29, K 10, I, G, H at 8, J 5, D 3, B 2, F 2, A 1)
- The Reaper appeared in the queue with no visible special effect. D offered to help the others score in return for special weapons.
- C killed Frankenstein, G the gargoyle, D the zombie (poisoned by E). J spent a grenade on the goblin without a kill. B set a time bomb.

Round 4 (order: F 38, B 21, C 14, I 12, H 11, G 10, K 10, A 6, D 5, J 5, E 5)
- The bloc offered trio Z the clown in a deal. E asked the dealer whether people could be killed and was told yes. E told I, thought of attacking D, put it off because D's position and the weapons available did not allow a sure kill, and warned K not to attack players this round.
- A then noticed the double lines at slots 10 and 11, held by J and E. D proposed hitting J; D (slot 9) and E (slot 11) each used a Dagger on slot 10. J, at 5 health, was killed; E took J's points and 2 pieces.
- B's time bomb killed the goblin and K killed the clown. A used ice on Medusa and H set a time bomb on that battlefield.

Round 5 (points order: B 27, F 26, D 24, K 22, A 16, C 16, I 12, H 11, G 10, E 3)
- K, then E, bought their way to the front, giving E, K, B, F, D, A, C, I, H, G.
- The bloc forgot A's ice was still on Medusa, so its early hits on Medusa did nothing.
- B's dagger and second time bomb killed the Reaper (with grenade damage from C and K already on it); F, who needed it to stay in the living quarters, missed out. H's dagger and time bomb killed Medusa.

### Result
- Final points: A, B, C 26.3 each; K 22; D, E, F 17.6 each; G, H, I 16 each; J 0.
- A, B and C won 2 pieces each.
- Pieces after the match: E 11, B 9, A 6, C 6, D 4, G 4, H 4, F 3, I 2, K 1, J 0.
- Living quarters: A, B, C, E, and D (chosen by E among D, G and H, tied at 4). Prison: F, G, H, I, K.
- J was eliminated; the prize mission succeeded (cumulative 180 million won).

### Differences from our version
- Show: 11 players, alliances fixed for the whole game; ours: 4 to 8 players (4 on the page), new partners chosen every round.
- Show: points set the attack order and can be moved between allies; ours has no attack order, attacks are simultaneous.
- Show: three monsters on battlefields with a queue, one weapon per turn, last hit takes everything; ours: one monster per round, any number of cards, the strongest alliance that reaches its strength takes the reward, shared by power played.
- Show: a Dagger that returns plus named special weapons won as loot; ours: 8 random strength cards and a point auction for an extra card.
- Show: the alliance splits its total equally at the end; ours shares each reward at once.
- Show: 5 starting points, 13 monsters; ours: 20 points, 6 monsters of strength 8 to 22.
- Show: hidden rule that players in slots 10 and 11 can be attacked and eliminated, plus the Scout and Change Order items; ours has none of these.

### Gaps
- The guide's round 1 attack table credits the tree monster kill with a grenade and dual swords, while the monster list and the holdings table show a grenade only.
- The goblin's loot is listed as a grenade, but the round record and piece changes suggest a piece as well.
- Player F's action in round 5 was not shown.
- The price of 1 piece for the items is read from an icon and confirmed by the piece changes.
- Whether the 6-piece prize wording covers only the top score is clear from the result, though the translation is rough.
- Whether a monster would fill a player battlefield after a player died never came up.

## Season 2 (Death Room), game-rules page
Source: the game-rules page has no block for this game; these points come from folded tables, footnotes and logs of the Day 3 page.

### Rules added
- A time bomb hits whatever stands on the battlefield when it goes off. If an attack has just killed a monster, the bomb strikes the newcomer, so two kills in one turn are possible (when the newcomer has 10 health or less).
- A lost Dagger is not replaced either.

### Corrections to the day record
- The goblin's loot was 1 piece and a grenade (round log and piece count), not a grenade alone. This settles the gap about the goblin.
- The clown's loot was 2 grenades: the round log says so, and its killer still held one after using one. The monster list names only "grenade".
- Tree monster loot, still open: the monster list and holdings table show a grenade only, the attack table grenade and dual swords. The killer later attacked with dual swords with no hand-over to them recorded, but another player's pair drops out of the holdings table without a note, so this does not settle it.
