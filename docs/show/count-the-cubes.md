## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 1: mini-game 1 of the main match (Either/Or)
- **Name in the source:** Counting Cubes (Count the Cubes)
- **Players:** 16, each for themselves
- **Stakes:** a priority order used to break ties in later parts of the match

### Rules on the show
- A cube problem is revealed; each player works out the number of cubes and enters it on a terminal.
- A dealer confirms the answer. A wrong answer locks that player out for 20 seconds.
- The mini-game ends when 15 players have answered correctly.
- The order of correct answers is the priority ranking; it favours the higher-placed player in later ties.

### How it went
- Player A was first to answer correctly and Player B second.
- Player C was struggling; B gave an indirect hint, and C finished 14th.
- Player D was the 15th and last correct answer; Player E was left unsolved.

### Result
- Priority order: A 1st, B 2nd, C 14th, D 15th, E 16th (the other places went to the remaining players).
- No points, pieces or elimination came directly from this mini-game.

### Differences from our version
- Ours is solo with 5 rounds of 20 seconds each; the show was a 16-player race with no time limit mentioned.
- Ours scores 100 plus a speed bonus; the show only recorded the finishing order.
- In ours a wrong answer loses the round; on the show it cost a 20-second lockout and the player could try again.

### Gaps
- The number of problems (apparently one), the shape of the stack, the answer and any overall time limit are not given.
