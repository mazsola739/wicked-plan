## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 1: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Heavy 13
- **Players:** 7 prisoners, each for themselves. Seats 1-7: Players A, B, C, D, E, F, G
- **Stakes:** 6th and 7th eliminated; 1st place gets 4 pieces (shared equally if tied, remainder discarded)

### Rules on the show
- Each player gets 7 rock cards numbered 0-6 and 7 chips: 2 red, 2 green, 2 blue and 1 rainbow.
- Three carrier stands (red, green, blue) hold weighted rock cubes.
- The first-player marker starts with the prisoner ranked highest in that day's main match (here D).
- Each round everyone secretly submits one card and one chip within 3 minutes. The chip names the stand, the card the weight of the cube.
- Chips are revealed first, then cards. Placing order: rainbow chip first, then higher card first; equals go clockwise from the first-player marker.
- A rainbow chip lets its player choose any stand at the moment of placing.
- In each round a player may pass once when their turn to place comes. Passed cubes are placed after everyone else's, in reverse order of passing (the first to pass places last).
- If a cube brings a stand's total weight to 13 or more, its owner takes all the cubes that were on the stand before it and gets one penalty point per cube taken. The new cube stays.
- The player who took the latest penalty starts the next round.
- After round 7 everyone receives a fresh set of cards and chips; the cubes stay on the stands. The game runs 14 rounds.
- Fewest penalty points wins. On equal points, whoever was penalised more recently ranks lower.

### Differences from our version
- Show: 3 stands (red, green, blue). Ours: 4 stacks (adds gold).
- Show: colourless cards 0-6 plus a separate colour chip. Ours: coloured cards 1-6, dealt 8 from a 48-card deck.
- Show: cubes are placed one by one in a set order. Ours: all plays resolve together.
- Show: 13 or more overloads; only the player who tips it is penalised, by the number of cubes already there. Ours: 13 is full and pays a bonus; over 13 penalises everyone who added that round by twice their card.
- Show has penalties only, lowest wins. Ours scores each card's value, highest wins.
- Show: a pass delays your placement within the round, once each round. Ours: a pass skips the round, 2 per game.
- Show: rainbow chip once per 7-round set, and it moves you to the front. Ours: once per game, no order effect.
- Show: 14 rounds, 7 players. Ours: 10 rounds, 3-6 seats.

### Gaps
- A sentence about the first player's position being freely chosen and then fixed is unclear (possibly seat choice).
- The translation renders the green and blue chips with unrelated words; the colours were matched using the round 3 description.
- The title speaks of not exceeding 13, while the rule text and the round log treat 13 itself as overload.

## Season 3 (Dead Again), game-rules page

The game-rules page has nothing on this game. The points below come from the folded tables of the Day 1 page showing each seat's unused chips and cards after rounds 1 to 6 and 8 to 13.

### Rules added
- None. The tables agree with the record: each round every player spends one chip and one card, and a set of 7 holds 2 red, 2 green, 2 blue and 1 rainbow chip with cards 0 to 6.
- Rainbow chip use, by seat (seats 1 to 7 as in the day record). First set: seat 3 in round 3, seat 7 in round 4, seats 4 and 6 in round 5, seat 5 in round 6, seats 1 and 2 in round 7. Second set: seats 1 and 3 in round 8, seat 4 in round 9, seats 5 and 6 in round 10, seat 7 in round 12, seat 2 in round 14.

### Corrections to the day record
- None. The colour matching holds: the two oddly translated chip words ("candle" and "green onion") are most likely the Korean short forms of green and blue, in that order, and the third short word is rainbow. This closes that gap in the day record.

### Gaps
- The tables do not show which stand each rainbow chip went to.
