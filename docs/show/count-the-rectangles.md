# Count the Rectangles on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/count-the-rectangles.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants

## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 1: mini-game 2 of the main match (Either/Or)
- **Name in the source:** Counting Squares (Count the Rectangles)
- **Players:** 16, as three teams of three and seven solo players (10 sides)
- **Stakes:** victory points; the results also fed the third choice of the match

### Rules on the show
- Before the game each player chooses (second choice of the match) to play in a team of three or alone. Teams cannot be changed or dissolved until the mini-game ends.
- A board of square cells, some containing numbers, is shown with a target number.
- Players must find all the rectangles whose enclosed numbers add up to the target.
- A player who has found them all presses the buzzer and states the answer within 10 seconds. A wrong answer bars that team from the rest of that problem.
- Each problem has a 5-minute limit. If nobody is right in time, or every team has answered wrongly, the next problem comes.
- A team with two correct answers has won and leaves the game.
- The game ends when half of the teams have won.
- Points: a winning team shares 7, a losing team loses 4 between its members; a solo winner gets +7, a solo loser -3. How a trio splits its points is set by the third choice:
  - winners (team majority): representative +5 and others +1 each, or representative +1 and others +3 each;
  - losers (representative decides): representative -3, or the two others -2 each.
- After the game each trio picks as representative a member who contributed much.

### How it went
- Player A, playing alone, was the first winner, with two correct answers in a row.
- The other four winning sides were one trio (Players B, C and D) and solo Players E, F and G.
- The winning trio had D as representative; B and C voted for the split that gave the two others more.
- The two losing trios, whose representatives were Players H and I, both chose to have the representative pay.
- After broadcast it came out that one accepted answer (G's first) had been judged wrongly.

### Result
- Winners: A, E, F, G (+7 each); trio: D +1, B +3, C +3.
- Losers: H -3, I -3, the three losing solo players -3 each; the other members of the losing trios 0.
- The solo players then made a further choice (extra challenge or -1 point), recorded under either-or.

### Differences from our version
- Ours: count every rectangle drawn by lines. Show: find the rectangles whose numbers add up to a target.
- Ours is solo, 5 rounds of 30 seconds with a typed count; the show used a buzzer race between teams and solo players, 5 minutes per problem.
- Ours scores by correctness and speed; on the show two correct answers won, and the game stopped once half the sides had won.

### Gaps
- Board size, numbers, targets and number of problems played are not given.
- The translation says "squares"; "rectangles" is assumed from the English title. It is unclear whether the answer is the count or the rectangles themselves.

## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 5: exile match 07
- **Name in the source:** Counting Squares ("Count the Rectangles")
- **Players:** 3 exiles, one of them just arrived from the prison match
- **Stakes:** elimination match; one player is out of the show for good

### Rules on the show
The exile page says the rules are those of the Day 1 mini game. From the Day 1 page:
- A board of square cells, some holding numbers, and a target number.
- Players find how many squares have inner numbers adding up to the target.
- A player who has found them all buzzes and gives the count within 10 seconds. A wrong answer locks that player (team) out of that problem.
- 5 minutes per problem; if time runs out or all answer wrongly, the next problem comes.

In the exile match each player played alone, and a player who answered a problem correctly was safe.

### How it went
- Round 1: Player A answered correctly first and was safe.
- Round 2: Player B saw that the problem had no way to make the target and called "0", and was safe.

### Result
A and B stay; Player C eliminated.

### Differences from our version
- Show: count the squares whose numbers sum to a target. Ours: count all rectangles drawn by the lines of a figure.
- Show: buzzer, one try per problem, answer may be 0. Ours: 5 rounds of 30 seconds, typed answer, 100 points plus a speed bonus.
- Show (exile): safe one by one until one player is left. Ours: solo score.

### Gaps
- The exile page does not say whether one correct answer was enough to be safe by rule or only here (on Day 1 a team needed two).
- Board sizes and targets are not given.
