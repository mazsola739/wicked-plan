Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants

## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 1: mini-game 3 of the main match (Either/Or)
- **Name in the source:** Laying Grass (Lay the Grass)
- **Players:** 16, each for themselves; optional pairs through one of the choices; open alliances
- **Stakes:** victory points

### Rules on the show
- One park board of 114 squares.
- Seven grass tile types, numbered 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 11 and 13, each a shape of several squares.
- **Tile selection:** one tile per player, chosen publicly, the player with the fewest points first (ties: better priority rank from mini-game 1 first). Several players may take the same type; a choice cannot be changed.
- **Placement:** players place one at a time, lowest tile number first (ties: better priority rank first). The tile may be rotated but not turned over, and must lie fully on the board.
- A player who places the tile scores its number; a player who cannot scores nothing.
- The game ends when everyone has had their turn.
- **Modifiers** (choices 6-8 of the match, made around this mini-game; choice 5 let a player preview them instead of taking 5 points):
  - Attack: double points for a placed tile, -4 if it cannot be placed. Defence: may remove up to 2 squares from your tile; two removed squares must be connected, numbered squares cannot be removed, no hole may be made inside the tile and the tile may not split.
  - Win-win: play as a pair and gain extra points equal to the number of edges where the two tiles touch, divided by agreement. Check: +5 if 9 or fewer players (yourself included) place successfully.
  - Street tree (one) or manholes (one +1 and one -1, single squares). Only tiles with a hole can be laid over a tree. Covering a +1 manhole adds a point, covering a -1 takes one.
  - Order on the board: manholes, then trees, then grass.
- Players had about 20 minutes to prepare before the laying began.

- A took the 9. Alliance members ahead of A in tie order took 9 too, but some who came after A copied them, which left them stuck behind in the queue. Much of the alliance could not place.

- Match points after this mini-game: P 34, L 30, N 29, O 29, J 21, H 20, I 20, K 18, B 11, M 11, G 6, D 5, E 5, F 5, C 2, A 1.
- No elimination yet; elimination was settled by later parts of the match.

### Differences from our version
- Show: one tile per player in the whole game, self-chosen from seven numbered types. Ours: 7 rounds with the same drawn piece for everyone.
- Show scores the tile's number (plus modifiers). Ours scores the largest connected patch and the largest solid square.
- Show forbids flipping; ours allows turning and flipping.
- Show: 16 players on a 114-square irregular park; ours: 1-4 players on a square field with rocks.
- Show has attack/defence, pairing, trees and manholes; ours has none of these.
- Show order depends on points and tile number; ours rotates the first player.

### Gaps
- Tile shapes and the board layout exist only as pictures; the tile numbered 4 is garbled in the translation.
- Not stated whether tiles must touch other tiles or whether the number equals the tile's size.
- Which pairs formed, where trees and manholes went and the exact make-up of each score are not given.
