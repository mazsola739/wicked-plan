# Remove One on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/minus-one.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 1: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Remove One
- **Players:** the 7 prisoners, each playing alone
- **Stakes:** the last player is eliminated; the top scorer of the final stage wins 1 piece.

### Rules on the show
- Everyone holds the same eight cards, 1 to 8.
- Each round, a player places two of their cards, one on the left and one on the right. All pairs are revealed.
- Each player then secretly marks which one of the two to play, and the choices are revealed.
- Among the numbers played by only one person, the lowest wins. The winner scores points equal to that number and gets one victory token.
- The played card is gone. The other card goes to a holding area and cannot be used in the next round only.
- 18 rounds at most. Card sets are reset after rounds 6 and 12.
- Settlements follow rounds 3, 6, 9, 12 and 18. At a settlement each remaining player adds 1 point per victory token held.
- Rounds 3, 6, 9 and 12: the single player with the most points is safe and leaves the game. Tie: more victory tokens; still tied: whoever won a round most recently.
- After a settlement the points of those still playing go back to zero; victory tokens are kept.
- Last stage: three players play rounds 13-18, then settle. The top two survive, the top scorer also gets 1 piece, and the third is eliminated.

### How it went
- Players A and B agreed on signals so as not to pick the same numbers; B won round 1 with a 1. C won round 2 with a 1.
- Round 3: D won with a 2 and became the first safe player (3 points at settlement).
- Rounds 4-6: E won with 1, F with 1, A with 2. A was second to get out.
- Rounds 7-9: E won with 1, F with 1, B with 1. B announced beforehand that a 1 was coming; C also held a 1 as an option, heard the announcement again and backed off, preferring to stay for the final piece. B, E and F tied on 3 points and 2 tokens; B got out as the latest winner.
- Rounds 10-12: G won with 1, C with 1, then C with 2. In round 12 E and C both had a 2 available; E played another card, which let C get out fourth.
- Rounds 13-18, between E, F and G: winners were F (1), E (1), F (3, the others cancelling on 2), G (1), G (3), F (2).

### Result
- Safe in order: D, A, B, C; then F and G from the last stage.
- F finished top and won 1 piece; G survived in second.
- E was eliminated, the first elimination of the season, and the piece E held was lost.

### Differences from our version
- 7 players with cards 1-8; ours seats 3-6 with cards 1-10.
- Up to 18 rounds with settlements where players leave safe one by one; ours is 7 rounds, everyone to the end.
- The winner scores their own number plus a token (worth 1 point at each settlement); ours scores the sum of all kept cards.
- The unplayed card is locked for one round; in ours it returns to the hand at once.
- Card sets reset after rounds 6 and 12; ours has no reset.
- Elimination and one piece as stakes; ours ranks by points.

### Gaps
- The settlement bonus wording is garbled; "1 point per victory token" is read from it and matches the settlement scores shown for rounds 3, 6 and 9.
- The score table for the settlements after rounds 12 and 18 is garbled; no final totals are stated.
- Time limits for showing and choosing are not given.
