## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 7: final, game 1
- **Name in the source:** Card Chess / Card Janggi
- **Players:** the 2 finalists
- **Stakes:** first of the three final games; two game wins take the final and the 200 million won

### Rules on the show
- Each player gets 1 large piece, 3 small pieces and 5 movement cards. Pieces begin on fixed starting squares.
- **Cards:**
  - Rat ("Mouse" in the translation): 1 square up, down, left or right.
  - Horse ("Word" in the translation): 2 squares straight, then turn and go 1 more.
  - Monkey: 1 square to any of the 8 neighbours.
  - Elephant: 1 or 2 squares in one diagonal direction.
  - Giraffe: 2 squares up, down, left or right.
- A coin toss decides who starts the first round; later rounds alternate.
- Each player lays their cards face down in the order they will be used. In the very first laying, the second player lays only 4.
- When the last card has been used, the player lays out 5 cards again within 3 minutes and turns up the first.
- After the first laying both turn up their first card at the same time.
- On a turn, move one piece as the open card says within 90 seconds, then turn up the next card.
- Moving onto an opponent's piece removes it.
- If time runs out, the opponent moves one of the player's pieces as they like, following that card.
- **Win a round:** capture the opponent's large piece, or bring your own large piece to the starting square of the opponent's large piece.
- Up to 3 rounds; the first to win 2 wins the game.
- Beforehand each finalist could practise for 30 minutes with one eliminated player of their choice.
