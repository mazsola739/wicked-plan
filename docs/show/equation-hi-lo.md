## Season 1, Day 6: main match
- **Name in the source:** Equation High-Low (machine translation also "Formula High Low").
- **Players:** 7, each for themselves, seated by lot: Players A, B, C, D, E, F, G with 20, 30, 30, 20, 20, 65 and 15 chips (4, 6, 6, 4, 4, 13 and 3 pieces).
- **Stakes:** pieces turned into chips. Losing all chips meant elimination from the show; the last three turned chips back into pieces.

### Rules on the show
- Every piece is exchanged for 5 chips.
- Each player holds the symbols +, − and ÷.
- Deck of 52: 44 number cards 0–10 in four colours (gold, silver, bronze, black, ranked in that order), 4 root cards and 4 multiplication cards.
- Each round: ante 1 chip, then 1 hidden card. A symbol card dealt as the hidden card is returned openly and redrawn until a number comes.
- Then 2 open cards. A root card brings one extra number card. A multiplication card lets the player discard one of the basic symbols (plus or minus in the records) and draw an extra number card. A symbol turning up on such an extra draw is thrown away and drawing goes on until a number comes.
- First betting round: call, raise or fold; checking is allowed until someone has bet. It ends when all players still in have put in the same. The most that can be bet is what the player with the fewest chips holds.
- Then 1 more open card; players arrange their formula and the final betting follows, run like the first.
- The root applies to one number. Formula time limit 90 seconds; no formula in time loses.
- After the final bet each player secretly picks a marker in the hand: **High** (closest to 20 wins), **Low** (closest to 1 wins) or **Swing** (both).
- The High winner and the Low winner each take half of the chips bet; an odd chip is discarded. When only one side is contested its winner takes the whole pot.
- **Swing:** show one formula, then rearrange and show the other. The player must win both sides or the bet fails; a player who beats the swinger then takes the whole pot.
- **Tie-break:** High goes to the player whose largest number card is higher, Low to the player whose smallest number card is lower; for equal numbers the card colour decides (higher colour for High, lower for Low).
- **From round 25:** High/Low choices are revealed at the same time.
- **End:** play goes on until 3 players remain. They get 1 piece per 5 chips, remainder discarded; a player with 4 or fewer chips still gets 1 piece.

### How it went
- In the morning the others loosely agreed to knock out F first, expecting F to have gained pieces in prison. A practice round and 30 minutes of free time came first. The starting player moved on by one seat each round.
- **Round 1:** C and G folded late. B and D both made exactly 1; B won Low on the card tie-break. F won High (16.29). B 49, F 84, D and E down to 7.
- **Round 2:** only B and C stayed to the end; C took High (19.2), B took Low.
- **Round 3:** A made exactly 1 for Low and F took High (18.94). E bet the last chips and lost: eliminated.
- **Round 4:** F took High, B Low.
- **Round 5:** F took High; B (1.5) beat G (0.43) for Low by a small margin. G, all in, was eliminated; a different order of the formula, or folding, would have saved G.
- **Round 6:** A took High with only 10, F took Low (1.125). D fell to 1 chip.
- **Round 7:** D, all in with that chip, won High (17.71); F took Low.
- **Rounds 9–11:** D won Low with an exact 1 in round 9 and High (19.5) in round 11, climbing to 10 chips; F kept growing (121). C fell to 1 chip. From round 9 the table reportedly agreed to keep first bets small.
- **Round 12:** A declared a Swing and won both sides (18.875 High, 1.1 Low), the only successful Swing of the match. C was eliminated.
- **Round 14:** all four went Low; D won the whole pot with 0.9.
- **Round 20:** D and F both made exactly 1; D won the tie-break and a large pot: D 44, F 95.
- **Round 22:** F declared a Swing (22 High, 1 Low); D's exact 1 beat F's on the tie-break, so the Swing failed and D took the whole pot: D 58, F 85.
- **Late game:** past round 25 and more than 7 hours in, choices were revealed simultaneously. A went all in on Low (−0.1) against B (1.25) and was eliminated.
- F, with about half of all chips from the start and a growing lead, played cautiously (on purpose, by F's own later account, to watch the alliance members fight each other). D recovered from a single chip.

### Differences from our version
- Show: 7 players until 3 remain; ours: 3–6 seats, 3 hands.
- Show: chips bought with pieces (15–65); ours: 20 each.
- Show: root and multiplication cards in the deck, with +, − and ÷ in hand; ours: number cards only, each player holds all four symbols and uses three.
- Show: 1 hidden card and the rest open, dealt in two steps around the first bet; ours: 2 up and 2 down at once.
- Show: free raises capped by the poorest player's chips, all-in possible; ours: raise by 2, at most 2 raises, no all-in.
- Show: side picked after the final bet, with Swing; ours: side picked while building, before the second bet, no Swing.
- Show: odd chip discarded, ties broken by cards; ours: odd chip to High, ties share.
- Show: 90-second formula limit; ours: none.

### Gaps
- Rounds 8, 10, 13, 15–19, 21, 24 and others near the end are left out; the number of the last round is not given.
- A further rule from round 25 may be hidden in a garbled phrase (something like a speed-up rule) besides the simultaneous reveal.
- The wording on which symbols a multiplication card lets you discard is garbled.
- The exact order of formula submission and the final bet is not fully clear.
- A few table entries are inconsistent (for example one error value in round 12 and one chip change in round 23).
- The source does not say what happens if two or more players drop out together and fewer than 3 remain.
