# Cooperative Puzzle on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/spinning-puzzle-wheel.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).

## Season 1, Day 1: prize match
- **Name in the source:** Cooperative Puzzle (1st prize match)
- **Players:** the 10 players not in prison, in numbered places around a rotating table
- **Stakes:** 50 million won for the prize pot if all 10 puzzles are solved; personal pieces for solvers

### Rules on the show
- 10 rounds. Each round a box with puzzle pieces and a target picture is put on the rotating table.
- All pieces given must be used to build the pictured shape.
- When the dealer opens the box, the table starts turning clockwise and player 1 starts on the puzzle. As it moves away, the next player takes over where the last one stopped.
- Time limit: 3 minutes per puzzle.
- When the puzzle is complete the table stops and the round ends; the player it is in front of is credited with the solve.
- A player credited with 2 puzzles gets 1 piece, and 1 more for each further puzzle.
- Solving all 10 wins the mission. One puzzle not finished in time fails it and ends the game at once.

### How it went
- Before the start, one player proposed using the personal rewards for the common good (for example for the prisoners); the idea was not adopted.
- Pieces per puzzle, rounds 1-8: 4, 4, 4, 10, 9, 10, 7, 7.
- Credited solvers: round 1 Player A, round 2 Player B, rounds 3 and 4 Player C, round 5 Player D, round 6 Player A, round 7 Player C.
- In rounds 1 and 6, B had worked out the answer, but the table had moved on to A, who finished it.
- Round 8 was not solved in time; rounds 9 and 10 were not played.

### Result
- Mission failed: no prize money.
- Pieces already earned were paid: A +1 (two solves), C +2 (three solves).
- Afterwards A gave one piece to B.

### Differences from our version
- 10 players against 1-4.
- Show: one puzzle at a time, ten in a row; ours: six open at once on the wheel.
- Show: the table turns continuously and players hand over as it passes; ours: 12-second turns with one action each.
- Show: 3 minutes per puzzle and one timeout ends everything; ours: 36 turns in all, unfinished runs still score.
- Show uses every piece; our trays hold one decoy.
- Show rewards the individual finisher with pieces; ours gives a shared team score and a bonus for turns left.

### Gaps
- Rotation speed is not in the rules; a player's remark suggests one turn per minute (about three chances each).
- The piece shapes and target pictures were images and are not described.
- The guide's piece tables disagree: one shows 2 pieces for D after the match, who solved only once; the history and the next day's table support no reward for D and 2 pieces for C.
