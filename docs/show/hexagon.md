How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/hexagon.md).

## Season 1, Day 7: final, game 2
- **Name in the source:** Hexagon.
- **Players:** the two finalists, Player A (more pieces) and Player B.
- **Stakes:** the second final game; A had won the first, so a win here gave A the season and ₩250 million.

### Rules on the show
- 19 panels in a hexagon with rows of 3, 4, 5, 4, 3. One face has a number, the other a letter A–S (A–C top row, D–G, H–L, M–P, Q–S bottom row).
- Each round the numbers are shown for 30 seconds; the panels then turn to the letters and the round's target number appears.
- Find three panels in a straight line whose numbers add up to the target.
- Press the buzzer, then call the three letters within 10 seconds.
- Right: +1 point. Wrong: −1 point. Calling an answer already given in the round, or running out of time, counts as wrong.
- Called panels are not turned over and found answers are not marked, so players must remember them too.
- The number of answers in a round is not told. A round ends when all are found or nobody buzzes for 90 seconds.
- 10 rounds, each with a new board and target. Most points wins; a tie goes to extra play.
- **Piece benefit:** the player with more pieces starts with 1 point.

### Result
- A won 7 : 5, taking the final 2–0 in games and the championship. The third game (Dice Poker) was not played.

### Differences from our version
- Show: a two-player buzzer duel; ours: solo.
- Show: 10 rounds with a new board each; ours: 5 rounds on one board.
- Show: several answers per round, +1 / −1, no repeats, 10 seconds to call; ours: one answer per target, 100 for a right one.
- Show: numbers shown for 30 seconds every round; ours: once, with a Ready button.
- Show: 1-point head start for the piece leader.

### Gaps
- The boards, targets and range of numbers are graphical and absent from the text.
- It is not stated whether the letters of an answer must be called in line order (the guide infers the middle panel must come in the middle).
- The round 10 wrong call is read from a garbled entry.
