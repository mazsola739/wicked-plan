## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 5: exile match 06
- **Name in the source:** Flags of the World
- **Players:** 3 exiles, one of them just arrived from the main match
- **Stakes:** elimination match; one player is out of the show for good

### Rules on the show
- Memorise national flags shown one after another, one per second (the source mentions 10 flags and a total of 30 seconds).
- Then mark the 5 correct ones among 10 options, in connection with a set of 25 choices (wording unclear, see Gaps).
- Look-alike flags (similar colour layouts, such as Ireland and Ivory Coast) are mixed into the options, so remembering the colour pattern is enough even without knowing the countries.
- Played over rounds; a player who succeeds is safe; ties go to overtime.

### How it went
Player A was safe first, very quickly. Player B and Player C were still level through round 3 and went to overtime, which B won.

### Result
A and B stay; C eliminated.
