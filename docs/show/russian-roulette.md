## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 2: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Russian Roulette
- **Players:** 6 prisoners; seats 1-6 in order: Players A, B, C, D, E, F
- **Stakes:** the two players hit are eliminated; each survivor gets 1 piece

### Rules on the show
- Seats are drawn by lot. Players are handcuffed to the table, so only neighbours can whisper; no writing material.
- Each player has a terminal and 16 credits; only the owner knows how many remain.
- One shared gun with 6 chambers, one facing each seat, 2 of them loaded in unknown places.
- Seat 1 starts; the starting seat moves clockwise each round. On a turn a player takes one action:
  - **Public investigation** (1 credit): choose 2 adjacent chambers; everyone learns whether each is loaded.
  - **Private investigation** (2 credits): the same, seen only by the player.
  - **Rotate one step** (3 credits) or **two steps** (4 credits), clockwise, in public.
  - **Manipulation** (6 credits are needed, never paid at once): secretly put in 1-3 credits. The player whose credits complete the six takes control and swaps the contents of 2 adjacent chambers; any excess is lost.
- Others cannot tell a private investigation from a manipulation payment.
- A player with no credits passes automatically. The game ends when 3 players have used all their credits.
- The gun then fires, going clockwise from the player it points at; the two players who get the loaded chambers are out.

### How it went
- At the start the loaded chambers faced seats 1 and 4.
- Seating favoured a four-person alliance (A, B, C, F) against D and E. E made a personal appeal that won over B and C, and the table switched to everyone for themselves, although A and F wanted to keep the alliance; F refused E any information.

| Round (first seat) | Public events | Revealed private actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (A) | A investigates: seat 1 loaded, seat 2 empty | B and C investigate privately |
| 2 (B) | E rotates two; A investigates | B pays 1 toward manipulation; F investigates privately |
| 3 (C) | A investigates | not shown |
| 4 (D) | F rotates one; A rotates two; C investigates two empty chambers | not shown |
| 5 (E) | A investigates two empty chambers | B pays 3; F investigates privately |
| 6 (F) | B rotates one; C investigates | A and D investigate privately; E pays 1, F pays 3 |
| 7 (A) | D rotates two; E rotates one | A pays 3 and completes a swap; B pays 3; C investigates privately; F pays 1 |

- The chamber checks show that one loaded chamber had also been moved between C's check in round 4 and A's check in round 5.
- B, D and F ran out of credits in round 7, ending the game.
- Late on, B went back to whispering information to A; C also shared information with B, D and E.
- D's and E's final rotations came after A's swap and set where the loaded chambers ended up.

### Result
- Hit and eliminated: C and F.
- Survivors, 1 piece each: A, B, D, E.
- Credits left: A 3, C 6, E 1; B, D and F 0.

### Differences from our version
- Show: nothing fires until the end; ours fires on every turn and a hit costs one of 2 lives.
- Show: exactly one action per turn; ours allows any number of actions followed by a pull.
- Show actions: public or private check of 2 adjacent chambers, rotation, shared six-credit swap. Ours: private peek at the next chamber, random spin, secret one-step tamper.
- Show: each chamber faces a particular player; ours uses a drum that only threatens whoever pulls, and it reloads.
- Show ends when three players are out of credits; ours when one player has lives left or after 6 pulls each.

### Gaps
- How the six manipulation credits are counted (shared total or per player, and when it resets) is unclear; the log cannot be reconciled with either reading from the shown actions alone.
- The earlier move of a loaded chamber is not marked in the log; who made it is not shown.
- Several private actions are unrevealed, and the final firing sequence is not described.
