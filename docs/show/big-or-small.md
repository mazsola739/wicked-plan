## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 7: final, game 1
- **Name in the source:** Big Small
- **Players:** the 2 finalists. Player A (14 pieces), Player B (11 pieces).
- **Stakes:** first of up to three games; two wins take the final. Piece benefit (for A): choose whether to be the dueller or the bettor in round 1.

### Rules on the show
- Each player holds number cards 0 to 10 and starts with 10 chips. The dealer keeps a separate set of cards 1 to 9 for each player.
- 18 rounds. In each round one player duels and the other bets; the roles swap every round.
- The dealer shuffles the dueller's set and turns one card up. The dueller sees it and plays one card face down.
- The bettor stakes chips on Big (the dueller's card is higher than the dealer's) or Small (it is lower).
- **Duel:** if the dueller's card is higher, the dueller gains chips equal to the difference. Otherwise the duel costs nothing.
- **Bet:** a right bet is paid back double (net gain: the stake), a wrong one is lost, and on a tie the stake is returned.
- All cards played, the players' and the dealer's, are discarded, so both card counts shrink.
- **End:** after round 18 the player with more chips wins; the game also ends at once if one player is reduced to 0.
- Players could take notes and count cards.

### How it went
- A chose to bet first, so A bet in odd rounds and B in even rounds (B had the last bet).
- Bets stayed small (1 to 3 chips) until the last round.

### Result
- Player B won 21 chips to 20 (final: B leads 1 to 0).

### Differences from our version
- Show: cards 0 to 10 for players, 1 to 9 for the dealer per player. Ours: 1 to 9 for both players and one dealer deck.
- Show: 18 rounds with alternating dueller and bettor. Ours: 9 rounds, both play and bet at once.
- Show: the dueller gains the difference when higher. Ours: no duel gain.
- Show: Big or Small, no stake cap seen. Ours: bigger, smaller or equal (double), stake 1 to 3.
- Show: ends early if a player has no chips. Ours: always after round 9.

### Gaps
- No minimum or maximum stake and no turn time limit are stated.

- Whether the bettor may skip a bet is not said.
