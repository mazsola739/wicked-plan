# Montage on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/duplicate-photo-buzzer.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).

## Season 1, Day 5: prize match
- **Name in the source:** Montage.
- **Players:** the six players in the living quarters, Players A–F, as one team.
- **Stakes:** ₩100 million added to the prize money on success; 1 piece per round to the fastest right answer.

### Rules on the show
- 5 rounds. Portraits of different people appear on a screen, one every 3 seconds.
- Players memorise them and press the answer button when a portrait repeats an earlier one.
- A press on a repeat clears the round; the fastest presser gets 1 piece.
- A press on a new portrait is wrong and adds 1 penalty point; the round goes on until the repeat is answered.
- The mission fails at once when the penalty total reaches 10, or when a repeat appears and nobody presses.
- The mission succeeds when all 5 rounds are answered.

### How it went
- Practice round: A answered right at the 15th picture (a repeat of the 2nd).
- Round 1: wrong presses by B (picture 9), C (13) and A (15, 19); D answered right at picture 28. Penalty total 4.
- Round 2: wrong presses by A (31) and E (32); B answered right at picture 34. Total 6.
- Round 3: A answered right at picture 28.
- Round 4: wrong press by E (22); A answered right at picture 24. Total 7.
- Round 5: A answered right at picture 25.

### Result
- Mission succeeded with 7 of the allowed penalty points; the prize money rose to ₩250 million.
- Pieces afterwards: A 6 (three rounds won), B 6, D 6 (one round each), E 4, F 3, C 2.
- Later in the living quarters A gave one piece each to C and F.

### Differences from our version
- Show: a team mission with a shared penalty limit; ours: a race for points.
- Show: portraits; ours: drawn pictures of shapes on a coloured ground, with near copies as traps.
- Show: 5 rounds, 3 seconds per picture; ours: 6 rounds, 1.4 s falling to 0.9 s.
- Show: a wrong press costs a team penalty point and play goes on; ours: −30 and locked out for the round.
- Show: a missed repeat fails the whole mission; ours: the round simply ends.
- Show: 1 piece to the fastest; ours: 100 points plus a speed bonus.

### Gaps
- The text does not say whether a repeat can come from an earlier round.
- Which earlier picture each repeat copied is mostly missing, and the number of pictures per round is not given.
- The translation of the added prize amount in the result line is garbled; the rules give ₩100 million.
