## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 2: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Time Auction
- **Players:** the 6 prisoners (one of them had just moved over from the living quarters)
- **Stakes:** the player with the fewest victory tokens is eliminated; the one with the most wins 1 piece.

### Rules on the show
- Everyone has a 10-minute time bank, counted to a tenth of a second.
- 19 auctions, one victory token each.
- Each player has a personal button hidden from the others. All hold it to get ready.
- A 5-second countdown follows. Letting go during it means sitting the auction out.
- After the countdown time is taken from every holder's bank until they let go. A display shows the auction time and stops when the last holder lets go; that player wins the token.
- Nobody is shown any remaining bank, not even their own: players must keep count.
- Only the winner is announced, not who else took part.
- An empty bank releases the button automatically, so nobody can overbid.
- Time spent is lost whether or not the auction is won.
- If two or more players tie to the tenth of a second, that token is discarded.
- After 19 auctions, most tokens wins the piece (tie: more time left). Fewest tokens is eliminated (tie: less time left).

- Before the start, A proposed that five players gang up on the newcomer B; C doubted it could work with D unconvinced. E, F and C instead formed a trio that split the auctions between them, one in three each. A, B and D played alone.

### Result
- Tokens: E 5, D 4, A 3, C 3, F 3, B 1.
- B was eliminated and B's piece was lost. E won 1 piece.
- Time left: C 14.0 seconds; B, D, E and F none; A's was not shown.

### Differences from our version
- 10-minute bank and 19 auctions; ours 100 seconds and 7 rounds.
- On the show nobody sees their own remaining time; ours shows your bank and hold time.
- The show displays the auction time, so the winning time is public; ours announces only the winner.
- Tenths of a second; ours counts 250 ms ticks.
- Elimination and one piece as stakes; ours ranks by tokens and time left, and gives points.
- Ours ends early if every bank is empty; the source mentions no such rule.

### Gaps
- The winner marks of the round table were lost in the page text; winners are worked out from the bids, which match every token total and the banks that add up to 10 minutes.
- The text says B won the first auction; the times show B's only token was the fourth.
- Some bids were not shown (D has about 2:16 spent in an unknown auction).
- The tie-break sentences are garbled; whether the piece is withheld on a complete tie is uncertain.
- A footnote suggests a vibration signal as time is spent; no rule is given.

## Season 2 (Death Room), game-rules page
Source: the game-rules page has no block for this game; these points come from a re-reading of the rule block and a footnote on the Day 2 page.

### Rules added
- A guide footnote states that it was not disclosed how a tie for last place would be settled between players who had all used up their time.

### Corrections to the day record
- Gap on the garbled tie-breaks: tie for most tokens, the player with more time left wins; if the time is equal as well, the piece is not paid out. Tie for fewest tokens, the player with less time left is eliminated.

### Gaps
- The wording about a complete tie for first place is still rough in translation.
