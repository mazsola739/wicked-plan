## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 3: prison match
- **Name in the source:** Piggy Bank
- **Players:** 4 prisoners, turn order by lot (Players A, B, C, D). Pieces at the start: A 2, B 3, C 3, D 2.
- **Stakes:** lowest-ranked player eliminated; first place 3 pieces (split if tied).

### Rules on the show
- 5 numbered piggy banks, each starting with 1 black and 1 white coin. In turn order each player gets one of banks 1-4 in the lower slot in front of them; the first player also gets bank 5 in the upper slot.
- **Turn:** put 1 to 5 coins (any mix of black and white) into the bank in the lower slot, truthfully announce how many of each colour went in, and hand that bank to any player. Then move your own upper bank down to the lower slot.
- The receiver puts the bank in their upper slot and has 20 seconds to state how many coins of one colour are inside it. The colour asked alternates between black and white.
- **Doubt:** the others then have 10 seconds to buzz. Only the fastest buzzer's doubt counts.
- No doubt: the receiver takes the next turn (drops coins and passes a bank).
- Doubt: the bank is opened and removed from the game.
  - Claim wrong: doubter +1, claimer -1.
  - Claim right: doubter -1, claimer unchanged.
- The player who lost the point gets a bank with a new number, becomes the starter and carries on.
- **Reset:** anyone may press a reset button at any time; it takes effect when 3 players have pressed it. All banks in use are removed and banks 1-5 are issued afresh with their starting coins.
- **Challenge:** before a reset happens, a player who has not pressed reset may buzz and guess the total number of black and of white coins in all banks in use. Right: +3. Wrong: -2.
- **End:** once someone reaches 5 points, players are ranked on the current scores; the game ends if the last place is then clear.
- Using coins to keep count during play is forbidden.

### How it went
- The first eight turns passed without a doubt, although one claim (turn 7) was wrong.
- Turn 9: Player D claimed 8 black; it was wrong, Player B doubted and scored. Interim: B +1, A 0, C 0, D -1.
- The match was then halted: Player C had sudden health symptoms and was treated by medical staff. After attempts to resume, Player C withdrew from the show on medical advice.

### Result
- Player C counted as the match's eliminated player (a medical withdrawal; unlike other eliminated players, C did not go to the exile location).
- Players A, B and D survived.
- The 3-piece first-place reward was apparently not paid to anyone; Player B led on points when play stopped.

### Differences from our version
- Ours has one shared bank and 6 rounds; the show used 5 numbered banks that stayed in play until opened or reset.
- Ours hides the colours of dropped coins and shows them to the receiver; on the show the dropper announced them truthfully, so it was a pure memory game.
- Drop size: ours 1-2 coins; show 1-5.
- Ours passes to the left; on the show the dropper chose the receiver.
- Ours fixes one counting colour per round; the show alternated the colour from claim to claim.
- Doubting: ours lets everyone doubt or trust, and a right claim costs every doubter; on the show only the fastest buzzer doubted, and a successful doubt also gave +1.
- No reset, no total-count challenge and no 5-point end in ours; ours opens a bank at 10 coins.
- No time limits in our rules page; the show had 20 s to claim and 10 s to doubt.
- Seats: ours 3-6; the show had 4.

### Gaps
- Rows 10-14 of the source's turn log are empty.
- The turn log does not fully agree with the stated bank rotation (the banks used on turns 4 and 7), and one declared count (turn 4) is garbled.
- The reset reissue is garbled in translation (it reads as two black coins per bank; the 1 black and 1 white start is assumed).
- Not said who starts after a reset, or what happens on a time-out.
