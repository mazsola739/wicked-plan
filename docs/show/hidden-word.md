# Hidden Word on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Wicked Plan has no version of this game.
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).

## Season 3 (Dead Again), Day 5: main match
- **Name in the source:** Hidden Word (5th main match)
- **Players:** 6, each for themselves; no alliance token in this game
- **Stakes:** 1st and 2nd to the living quarters, 3rd to 5th to prison, 6th eliminated; 1st also gets 4 pieces

### Rules on the show
- Before round 1 each player takes one of six envelopes, each holding five common (not proper) nouns.
- For round 1 each player picks one of the five as their hidden word; for round 2, one of the other four.
- Two rounds. In each round every player is the questioner once; the order is drawn by lot.
- A turn alternates "give hints" and "guess".
- **Hints:** the questioner offers words related to the hidden word (Korean noun forms only). After each, an AI score shows how similar it is to the hidden word.
  - Up to 10 hint words in 15 minutes.
  - A hint scoring 95% or more ends the hint phase at once.
  - If none of the 10 reaches 95%, the most similar word is revealed and the phase ends (see Gaps).
- **Similarity:** a language model trained on a large text corpus, combining closeness of meaning, how often the words appear in the same context, and how the words are related structurally.
- **Guessing:** within 5 minutes every other player writes one word; all are revealed together. Paying 1 piece buys one extra written word (only one piece per word).
- A player who guesses the word gets 1 point, and the next player becomes questioner.
- If nobody guesses, the questioner scores and goes on to another hint phase. At most three hint phases per word; the questioner's score is 1 for the first, 2 for the second, 3 for the third.
- All points are doubled in round 2.
- The game ends after round 2; ranking by points.

### How it went
- A practice turn came first. The practice questioner's hints and scores were: osmotic pressure 48.5, hardness 54.0, wind 78.5, water 78.5, reprimand 2.5, acorn 78.5, mushroom 82.0, pine tree 96.5. The answer was "tree"; one player wrote it, the others wrote "pine cone" or missed.
- Question order drawn: A, B, C, D, E, F.
- Sample word sets from the source: A held raincoat, painter, airport, swimming, waiting; E held milk, chef, bookstore, conversation, abalone.
- A did well in round 1 (the hint "heat" for the word "waiting" was praised), then faded.
- B led from early on (for example by guessing "raincoat") and also read opponents' faces and gestures to get words they were trying to keep hidden.
- C was at the bottom from the start and was out of reach by the middle of the match.
- E, who had just returned with 10 pieces, spent them freely on extra guesses but stalled from mid-game.
- D and F fought for the second living-quarters place; it went to F by one point after a correct last answer, with D misplacing the final question.

### Gaps
- The round-by-round log is empty in the source.
- The scoring wording is unclear after machine translation: it does not say whether the questioner also scores when someone guesses, or whether several correct guessers each score. It also reads as if the questioner is paid for staying unguessed, while the 95% limit ends the hints early; the questioner's incentive for good hints is not explained.
- "The most similar word is revealed" after 10 hints: presumably the best of the hints, but not stated clearly.
- Pieces of the players other than the winner are not given.
