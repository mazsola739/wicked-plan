# Word Tower on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/word-block-tower.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).

## Season 1, Day 3: prize match
- **Name in the source:** Word Tower
- **Players:** the 7 players not in prison, one cooperative team
- **Stakes:** 100 million won added to the prize money; on failure, each player without a finished word returns 1 piece

### Rules on the show
- Three rounds. At the start of a round its theme and a pile of letter cubes are revealed.
- Each player builds a word fitting the theme from the cubes, on their own mat.
- Within a 10-minute limit the team must make as many words as there are players (here 7), using every cube.
- Success opens the next round with a new theme and new cubes; completing round 3 completes the mission.
- Running out of time ends the game at once.
- Seats were chosen freely.

### How it went
- Players A and B, who had experience abroad, sat facing each other so they could help those around them.
- Round 1, countries (39 cubes): done in about a minute, with B quickly spotting the harder words. Final words: INDIA, JAPAN, BRAZIL, QATAR, CANADA, SPAIN, FINLAND. About 9:01 left.
- Round 2, fruit (39 cubes): five words came fast (ORANGE, COCONUT, KIWI, PEACH, PEAR). Player C found AVOCADO, which needed another O, so ORANGE was taken apart. Player D proposed GUAVA; PEACH was later replaced by CHERRY. GRAPE came with about 2 minutes left, and Player E found the last word, DURIAN. Final words: CHERRY, DURIAN, GRAPE, AVOCADO, PEAR, KIWI, COCONUT. About 1:38 left.
- Round 3, animals (38 cubes): FOX, WOLF, PIG, ZEBRA and TURTLE came fast; with about 5 minutes left the team was stuck. B found GIRAFFE and A found PENGUIN, but the spare letters did not form a word. They broke up TURTLE; in the last minute B asked whether insects counted as animals, and A and D made BUTTERFLY. Final words: WOLF, ZEBRA, GIRAFFE, PENGUIN, FOX, BUTTERFLY, PIG. About 17 seconds left.

### Result
- All three rounds succeeded.
- Prize money rose by 100 million won to 150 million won.
- No piece changes.

### Differences from our version
- Show: 7 players working all at once on one pile, free to take words apart and rebuild; ours 1 to 4 players, one word per turn, with an explicit take-back.
- Show: 10-minute limit; ours has no timer in the rules page.
- Show: themes countries, fruit, animals; ours draws 3 of 6 topics (no countries).
- Show: words up to at least 9 letters; ours 3 to 7 letters from a built-in list.
- Show: pass or fail, with a piece penalty and prize money; ours scores 300 per round minus 30 per take-back.

### Gaps
- That the 10 minutes apply per round is inferred from the remaining times; the rule text only says "within 10 minutes".
- Whether cubes belonged to particular players or could be freely shared is not stated.
