# Doubt and Bet on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/colour-count-bluff.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 6: main match
- **Name in the source:** Doubt and Bet (also "Doubtful Betting")
- **Players:** 5, each for themselves (alliances formed): Player A to Player E, lettered in the first round's turn order.
- **Stakes:** semi-final. The game ends with 3 players left; the one with the most pieces goes straight to the final, the other two play a prison match for the second place in the final. The two knocked out are eliminated.

### Rules on the show
- **Cards:** red, yellow, green, blue, purple and rainbow. The guide's probability note gives 60 cards, 10 of each kind.
- **Slots:** every player starts with 5 card slots and 4 slot chips. At each deal a player gets one hidden card per slot. Players bring their own pieces as betting currency.
- A slot chip can be staked; losing it removes one slot. The last slot cannot be staked. A slot can be bought for 1 piece, up to a maximum.
- **Order:** the first starter is drawn by lot. Later rounds start with the player who lost the previous doubt; after an elimination, the player just before the eliminated one in turn order starts. Every 10 rounds seats are redrawn.
- **Claim:** within 3 minutes name a colour (not rainbow) and a count from 1 to 20: how many cards of that colour are on the table. Rainbow cards count as the named colour. Each claim carries a bet of at least 1 piece or slot chip.
- **Answer:** only the next player answers, with accept or doubt.
  - Accept: the previous claimer takes their bet back, and the accepter now makes a claim with a higher count; the colour may change freely.
  - Doubt: the doubter calls or raises the claimer's bet; the claimer may call, raise or fold.
- **Fold:** the other side wins everything staked; no cards are shown.
- **Showdown** (bets matched): all cards are revealed. If the named colour plus rainbows is below the claimed count, the doubter wins; if equal or higher, the claimer wins. The winner takes all staked pieces and slot chips.
- After a result the round ends and new cards are dealt.
- **Fee:** from round 15, every 5 rounds each player pays 1 piece.
- **End:** when 3 players remain. Each slot then turns into 1 piece, added to the player's pieces.

### How it went
- Pieces at the start: A 12, B 7, C 4, D 2, E 8. A and E, seated together, formed a pair against B, C and D; B turned down A's offer to join the pair.
- Partners passed pieces to each other on purpose, for instance by claiming 20 and letting the partner doubt.
- Round 1 to 10: mostly transfers inside the two groups. In round 10 B, urged by D, doubted A's claim of 8 yellow; the cards held it. The new seat draw put A next to E again, and D told B and C that each should play for themselves.
- Rounds 11 to 20: D bluffed with all-ins against C. C folded once (round 11) and lost a piece (round 12). In round 14 C called an all-in of D, lost and was left with 1 piece, facing the fee at round 15. D then helped by passing C a piece. A and E kept accepting each other. In round 19 C risked everything on a claim of 10 yellow, lost and was eliminated.
- Rounds 21 to 30: A and E sat together again. B joined their side to survive (round 22). In round 23 the pair showed D a card setup meant to steer D's doubt; D deliberately doubted B instead and lost a piece, rejecting the arrangement. From then on it was 3 against 1.
- Rounds 31 to 39: seats again favoured the pair. After round 39 D asked who was meant to finish first and learned that E too was playing for A, who had given E pieces.
- After the round 40 fee: D 1, B 1, E 2, A 8. In round 41 A bet 2 and folded to move pieces to E (A 6, E 4).
- Round 42: E claimed 2 after A's 1, and D doubted with an all-in of all 5 chips on purpose, offering E first place so that A would drop into the prison match. E was torn; A said E could take it. E called and won, after about eight hours of play.

### Differences from our version
- Show: pieces and slot chips are bet on every claim. Ours: no betting; 3 lives each.
- Show: cards per player equal the slots owned (5 at the start). Ours: 4 cards each.
- Show: five colours plus rainbow wild cards. Ours: red, blue, green, yellow, no wild card; a 40-card deck.
- Show: the answer is accept or doubt; accepting requires a higher count. Ours: bet or call; the same count with a later colour also raises.
- Show: a doubt starts a call, raise or fold exchange. Ours: a call is resolved at once.
- Show: seats redrawn every 10 rounds, a fee from round 15, ends at 3 players. Ours: no fee; ends at one player or after 12 rounds.

### Gaps
- The slot-buying rule is garbled: price 1 piece is stated, the maximum is not.
- The guide says a won slot chip is converted into one piece, yet its tables count won slot chips as slot chips until the end; the totals are the same either way.
- The size of raises and any cap are not described.
- Exactly when a player is out is not stated; the eliminated players had no pieces and no bettable slot chips left.
- Whether the 5-round fee can be paid with a slot chip is not said.
- Many rounds were cut from the broadcast (24 to 27, 31 to 34 and others).
- The round 1 bet figures in the table look mistranscribed.
