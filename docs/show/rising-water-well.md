# Rising Water Well on the show

How this game was played on The Devil's Plan. Our version: [rules page](../games/rising-water-well.md).
Recorded from the NamuWiki episode guides (machine-translated), in our own words. Contestants
are Player A, B …; letters are assigned separately in each section. **Gaps** lists what the source
leaves open. Index: [On the show](README.md).
A **game-rules page** section comes after the day sections: where it adds to or corrects
them, it wins.

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 2: prison hidden stage
- **Name in the source:** Gam-dong (prison wing) Hidden Stage
- **Players:** 1 volunteer from the prison, Player A
- **Stakes:** failure eliminates the challenger, and another player may try again after 24 hours; success closes the stage. The reward is not stated on this page.

### Rules on the show
- Entry: the hidden manhole found and opened on Day 1. Only one person may go in; the cover is shut behind them, a wall opens and an underground passage leads on.
- A dealer guides the player to the inside of a well.
- A screen explains the mission under the keyword "Find the key" and a 10-minute countdown starts.
- Water pours into the well from above while the player searches.
- Some bricks in the well wall carry patterns and can be pressed.

### How it went
- A hesitated, feeling it was too early and having prepared for a knight's tour puzzle; the other prisoners talked A into going.
- A, surprised to find a different task, found that the patterned bricks could be pressed but was hampered by the falling water.
- The 10 minutes ran out.

### Result
- Failed. A was eliminated, the second elimination of the season, and A's piece was lost.

### Differences from our version
- The show asks for a key to be found by working the patterned wall bricks; ours is four picture riddles with number answers that open four locks.
- Ours takes 30 seconds off for a wrong answer and has Give up; neither appears in the source.
- Ours gives points; the show's stake is elimination.
- Shared: a well, rising water and a 10-minute limit.

### Gaps
- The solution of the stage, what the bricks do and where the key is are not explained.
- The reward for success is not stated.

## Season 2 (Death Room), Day 3: hidden stage (prison quarters)
- **Name in the source:** a hidden stage in the prison wing, with the on-screen task "Find the key" (a well that fills with water)
- **Players:** one, alone (Player A). The guide says another player had entered it the day before and was eliminated.
- **Stakes:** success paid 10 pieces from a safe; the guide indicates that failing meant elimination.

### Rules on the show
- The player is shut in a well; a small monitor explains the stage.
- A key must be found, put into the keyhole next to the monitor, and used to open a safe.
- Time limit: 10 minutes. Water pours in from the top and rises while the clock runs.
- The hint is the phrase "Find the key". The wall of the well has bricks with pictures on them that can be pressed and pulled.
- The solution: press, for each letter of the phrase, the brick whose picture is an English word starting with that letter (fox, ice, nose, dog, tree, heart, elephant, knife, elephant, yellow). This opens a hiding place near the bottom that holds the key.

### How it went
- Player A first pressed bricks at random, then worked out the first-letter rule from the fox and ice pictures.
- Some pictures could be read as more than one word, and Player A misread at least one.
- As the water rose, the low bricks became hard to see and Player A had to dive several times.
- With the time almost gone, Player A dived once more, took the key and opened the safe.

### Result
- Success: Player A received 10 pieces, went back to the prison wing with 11, and gave 1 of them to a fellow prisoner who had only 1.

### Differences from our version
- The show had one task (find one key through a letter-and-picture code); ours has four picture riddles in a row, one per lock.
- The show's code uses first letters of English words; ours has counting, tally, scale, symbol and clock riddles with number answers.
- No wrong-answer time penalty is reported on the show; ours takes 30 seconds off.
- The show's reward was 10 pieces, all or nothing, with elimination at stake; ours scores points per riddle and keeps them on failure.
- Both use a 10-minute limit.

### Gaps
- The number of bricks and the full set of pictures are not given.
- Whether the bricks had to be pressed in the order of the letters is not stated.
- The failure condition is not part of the quoted rules; it comes from the guide's commentary.

## Season 2 (Death Room), game-rules page
Source: the hidden-stage part of the game-rules page, plus a footnote on the Day 3 page. It covers the Day 2 and Day 3 prison hidden stage.

### Rules added
- **Route in:** a plate in the middle of the prison wing covers a manhole lid with eight evenly spaced holes. Water poured into a central hole marked with a Chinese character floats up a ball bearing a riddle (something always on its way that never arrives). The answer, "tomorrow", is entered letter by letter at the holes and opens the lid.
- Once one player is inside, the lid is shut from outside and a long sewer-like passage opens to the stage.
- **Stage notice:** one entrant only; it tests courage against fear and calls for a cool head. Success closes the stage. Failure eliminates the challenger, and another player may try after 24 hours.
- A producer remark cited in a footnote: the water flow was preset to reach collarbone height at the end of the 10 minutes.

### Corrections to the day record
- Day 3, "failing meant elimination" and the gap saying this rests on commentary: it is part of the stage's own notice.
- Day 2, gap "the reward for success is not stated": the entry rules add that success closes the stage; the reward itself (10 pieces) is in the Day 3 record.

### Gaps
- The game-rules page does not describe the puzzle inside the well; it refers to the Day 3 page.
- The notice does not name the reward.
- Whether the bricks had to be pressed in the order of the letters is still not said.
